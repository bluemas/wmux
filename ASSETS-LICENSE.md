## The Mochi crew bot characters

The wmux bot characters (the "Mochi crew"): their artwork, character designs,
colours as applied to them, and their motion.

- `src/renderer/components/Room/mochiCharacters.tsx`: the character artwork.
- `src/renderer/components/Room/botAvatar.css`: their motion (the
  `wmux-mo*` and `wmux-mochi-*` rules).
