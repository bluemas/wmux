### Added

- **Moa has an autonomy level wmux enforces, and a goal you approve once.** Settings › Moa now sets Moa's level from 0 (observe only: every call that would change something is refused) to 2 and 3 (delegate). At level 2 Moa can propose a goal: what to do, in which repository and workspaces, with a task, time and turn budget. You approve it with one card that shows everything it grants. Inside an approved goal Moa fans out, answers and instructs its own tasks, and hands work to the goal's workspaces without asking each time. Push, pull requests, merges, releases, secrets, deletes and permission prompts stay yours. Before this, Moa's level was only a sentence in its prompt. (#2025)
- **Workers a goal starts run with a restricted profile.** They run on Claude Code only. Push, PR, tag, release, publish and recursive-delete commands are denied in their session, and GitHub credentials are withheld from their environment (this adds friction; it is not a sandbox). The fan-out worker permission mode is pinned when you approve the goal. Fan-outs outside a goal keep your current settings. (#2025)
- **What a goal does not let through.** Under a goal, a hand-off that comes from a wake (worker output, PR comments, another PC's Moa) still asks you with a card, and work sent by another PC's Moa is never fanned out on the goal's budget. wmux also checks what Moa sends a worker (messages, fan-out prompts, task updates, channel posts) for requests to push, release, expose a secret or delete. That check is a tripwire, not a guarantee. (#2025)
